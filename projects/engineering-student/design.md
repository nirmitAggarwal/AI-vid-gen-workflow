# Design — engineering-student

**Style:** `marker-doodle` (new style, created in `video-design-types/marker-doodle/`)

## Mood

A smart friend explaining your life on the back of a notebook. Funny, fast and
warm, never mean — the jokes target the system, not the student. The paper
texture keeps it human; the red pen does the punching.

## Palette

- Paper `#F7F1E3`, sheets `#FFFDF6`, aged paper `#EDE3CB`
- Ink `#141414`, pencil `#6F675A`
- Highlighter `#FFD400` — one keyword per scene, always behind the word
- Red pen `#FF4B3E` — stamps, cross-outs, corrections, punchlines
- Ballpoint `#2050FF` — browser/phone/UI chrome only

## Typography

Two families: Impact/Arial Black for display (uppercase, 900, tight 0.94
line-height) and Helvetica 700 for spoken-caption body text. SF Mono/Consolas
for UI labels, counters, timers, page captions. Heavy weights only — no light
type anywhere.

## Layout

16:9 two-zone composition: type column on one side (safe margin 96px), doodle
on the other, alternating sides every 2–3 scenes. Mono page captions
`FIG. 01`–`FIG. 30` top-left sell the notebook feel and cover the cuts.

## Motion

Draw-on is the signature: every illustration is stroke-revealed with dash
offsets (10–14f, 2–3f stagger). Highlighter wipes land after the word, stamps
slam in 5f and jolt the frame 4px, sticky notes spring in with rotation,
cross-outs are two quick red strokes. Exits are hard cuts, never fades.

## Sound/edit rhythm

Audio at 1.1× with a dry, deadpan delivery. Punchlines get their own line and
their own scene; the edit never adds a beat the narrator doesn't have. No music
bed, no SFX — the voice is the track, the drawings keep time.
