# Storyboard — engineering-student

**Frame:** 1920×1080 @ 30fps · **Total:** 98.3s / 2949 frames · **Style:** marker-doodle
**Look:** warm paper + dot grid, thick black marker lines, highlighter yellow, red pen, mono notebook captions.

Global rules for every scene:

- Paper dot-grid background; no full-frame dark fills. One dark block allowed per scene max (e.g. a blackboard).
- Mono page caption top-left, `FIG. 01` … `FIG. 30`, at 24px, `textSecondary`, pasted at a slight angle.
- Entrances land in the first 25% of the line; exit is a hard cut (no fades to black). Stamps jolt the whole frame 4px for 3 frames.
- Rohan is a doodle: side-parted swoopy hair, round glasses, hoodie, permanent eye-bags. 12–20 strokes, never a rendered portrait.
- Type column alternates left/right every 2–3 scenes. Type never crosses x=900 (left) or x=960 (right); illustrations may bleed off-frame.
- `line.text` is never repeated verbatim as a paragraph — only keywords and punchlines go on screen.
